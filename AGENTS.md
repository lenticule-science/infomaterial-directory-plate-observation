# Infomaterial Directory Plate Observation

This settlement is one complete shallow observation die. It reads one directory inode
and the names, kinds, and metadata of its direct mounts. It never reads a child
interior and never recursively observes material.

Its placement-neutral plate identity commits to directory mode and the complete
ordered direct name/kind set. Its observation receipt separately states the
local access path and vector-time event. A downstream cover assembler may use
the plate with independently produced child material receipts on any machines.
Completeness is determined by the finite plate itself; callers do not impose a
mount-count ceiling.

It does not schedule work, observe child contents, assemble covers, infer
circuits, or certify enterprises.
